# HomeScope GTA Site Visitor Guide

This guide is for people using HomeScope GTA to explore listings, compare neighbourhoods and schools, save homes, request more details, and plan next steps.

## 1. What You Can Do on HomeScope GTA

HomeScope GTA helps you:

- search GTA homes for sale and lease
- open detailed listing pages with photos, MLS numbers, facts, descriptions, and tools
- save homes and compare them later
- save searches and turn on listing alerts
- search schools and see nearby homes
- use buyer and renter guides
- estimate mortgage payments and land transfer tax
- request a private showing
- ask for more details about a listing
- use the HomeScope assistant for help navigating the site

## 2. Search Listings

Use the listings page to browse available homes.

Listings page:

- `/listings`

You can search and filter by:

- city or market
- sale or lease
- price
- bedrooms and bathrooms
- property type
- address
- MLS number
- school area when available

Open a listing card to see the full listing page.

## 3. Listing Detail Pages

Each listing page can include:

- property photos
- price
- address
- MLS number
- city
- property type
- bedroom and bathroom count
- square footage range when available
- property description
- mortgage payment calculator for sale listings
- related buyer or renter guides
- Save button
- Book a Private Showing button
- Ask for Details button

Use the listing detail page when you want to review a specific property before taking action.

## 4. Ask for Details

Use Ask for Details when you have a question about a listing but are not ready to book a showing.

You can ask about:

- the property
- pricing
- MLS number
- neighbourhood
- schools
- documents
- next steps
- anything specific you want clarified

You will be asked for your email so HomeScope GTA can reply. After submitting, you will see a confirmation message on the listing page.

## 5. Book a Private Showing

Use Book a Private Showing when you want to request a visit to a property.

The showing request form may ask for:

- your name
- email
- phone number
- preferred showing date and time
- whether you agree to receive text messages
- listing and MLS details
- additional notes

After submitting a showing request, it appears in your dashboard under My Showings.

## 6. Save Homes

Use the Save button on listing cards or listing pages to keep homes for later.

Saved homes are available in your dashboard when you are signed in.

Saved homes help you:

- return to listings you liked
- compare homes
- keep notes
- review options before contacting HomeScope GTA

## 7. Saved Searches and Alerts

After filtering listings, you can save the search and turn on email alerts.

Saved search alerts help you keep track of new matching listings.

Alert options may include:

- daily alerts
- other frequencies when available

You can manage saved searches and alert settings in your dashboard.

## 8. Dashboard

The dashboard is your personal area on HomeScope GTA.

Dashboard page:

- `/dashboard`

Use it to manage:

- saved homes
- saved searches
- listing alerts
- private notes
- showing requests

Sign in is required for dashboard features.

## 9. School Search

Use the school search page if school-area research matters to your home search.

School search page:

- `/schools`

You can search by:

- school name
- board
- program
- municipality
- school level

You can sort school results by:

- rating, high to low
- rating, low to high
- name, A to Z
- name, Z to A

The school search page shows paged results so you can continue past the first set of schools.

Important: school boundaries and eligibility can change. Always verify school assignment, transportation, and eligibility directly with the school board before making a decision.

## 10. School Profile Pages

School profile pages can help you review:

- school details
- board and program information
- rating information when available
- nearby homes
- local context

Use school pages as a research starting point, not as an official school-placement confirmation.

## 11. Map Search

Map search helps you explore listings geographically.

Map search page:

- `/map-search`

Use it when location, nearby schools, commute, or neighbourhood boundaries are important to your search.

## 12. Tools

The tools area gathers practical search and planning tools.

Tools page:

- `/tools`

Common tools include:

- mortgage payment calculator
- land transfer tax calculator
- map search
- saved search alerts
- saved home comparison

## 13. Mortgage Payment Calculator

Use the mortgage calculator to estimate monthly carrying costs for a sale listing.

Mortgage calculator page:

- `/tools/mortgage-calculator`

It can help estimate:

- mortgage amount
- monthly principal and interest
- down payment impact
- amortization scenarios
- carrying cost assumptions

This is only an estimate. Confirm final numbers with a mortgage professional.

## 14. Land Transfer Tax Calculator

Use the land transfer tax calculator to estimate one of the major closing costs for Ontario purchases.

Land transfer tax calculator page:

- `/tools/land-transfer-tax-calculator`

Toronto purchases may have both Ontario and municipal land transfer tax.

## 15. Guides

The guides section explains common Ontario buyer and renter steps.

Guides page:

- `/guides`

Current guide topics include:

- first-time home buying in Ontario
- documents needed to buy a home
- organizing real estate documents
- Ontario rental application Form 410
- buying process
- leasing process
- lease document checklist
- mortgage payment calculator guide

## 16. Contact Page

Use the contact page for general questions that are not tied to one listing.

Contact page:

- `/contact`

Use listing-specific Ask for Details when your question is about a specific MLS listing.

## 17. Ask HomeScope Assistant

The Ask HomeScope button appears on the site as a floating chat helper.

It can help you:

- find the right page
- understand how saved searches work
- learn where to manage alerts
- find calculators and guides
- understand listing, school, and dashboard features

It is a site helper, not legal, financial, mortgage, tax, or school-board advice.

## 18. Privacy and Email Notes

When you submit a form, HomeScope GTA may save the details needed to respond or operate the website.

This may include:

- your email
- your phone number if you provide it
- the listing or MLS number
- your question or showing request details
- saved search and alert preferences

Check your junk or spam folder if you are expecting an email reply.

## 20. Good Search Workflow

1. Start with Listings or Map Search.
2. Open homes that look relevant.
3. Save homes you want to compare.
4. Use school search if schools matter.
5. Use calculators to estimate costs.
6. Save the search and turn on alerts.
7. Ask for Details when you have questions.
8. Book a Private Showing when you are ready to visit.
